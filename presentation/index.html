The problem · why cross-chain

The biggest AML blind spot in 2026 is the bridge.

The moment funds cross a bridge, the trace breaks. The deposit on the source chain and the withdrawal on the destination are separate transactions on separate ledgers, with no on-chain record joining them.

Bank AML

Sees fiat only.

Crypto analytics

Sees a single chain only.

Bridges

Nobody sees both sides.

Bridges have already replaced mixers as the primary laundering path. — crypto.news (2026) · Global Ledger (2026)

08 / 11
Honest limits

What this proves, and what it does not.

Addresses only

Travel Rule originator / beneficiary identity is off-chain and not in the OFT packet. This is a mechanism proof, not real KYC data.

Public labels only

Risk judgment is bound to public label sets, so it catches direct bridging. Sophisticated, layered laundering slips through.

Adoption-gated

The veto works only while our DVN is configured as required. It is an adoption problem, not an approval one.
